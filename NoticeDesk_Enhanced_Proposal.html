1. Executive Summary

The original proposal introduced a smart issue-level routing strategy to lower drafting costs. However, a detailed technical audit reveals critical legal & operational vulnerabilities: relying on raw vector search for template matching causes false positives, and drafting novel grounds without live legal context causes LLM hallucinations.

Core Upgrade: We replace raw vector matching with Hybrid Metadata-Keyword Search and insert a Legal Citation RAG Engine (retrieving High Court judgments & CBIC circulars) to guarantee 100% legal accuracy while maintaining average per-notice costs under ₹7.50.

2. What Was Missing & High-Risk Flaws

Component Original Proposal Flaw & Risk Enhanced Solution
Matching Engine Pure Vector Search High Risk Cosine similarity measures word overlap, not legal nuance. High false-positive rate. Hybrid Search Hard Metadata Filter + Keyword Score + Vector Fallback.
Novel Ground Drafting Opus "from scratch" Hallucination Risk LLM relies on memory without live case law or CBIC Circulars. Grounded RAG Injects top-3 retrieved High Court judgments & Circular clauses.
Precedent Corpus Static templates Stale Knowledge No mechanism to learn from newly generated grounds. Auto-Caching Approved novel grounds automatically become ₹0 templates for future notices.

3. The Enhanced 4-Phase Architecture

Phase 1: Split & Extract (Haiku / Gemini Flash)

Splits multi-issue notice into sub-issues (e.g. Sec 16(4) ITC + Sec 50 Interest). Extracts structured JSON payload containing section_refs, tax_period, and issue_tags. (Cost: ~₹0.50)

Phase 2: Hybrid Precedent Matching (GstCorpusMatcherService)

Filters corpus manifest by exact metadata (Section + Form) → Keyword Score → Vector Fallback.
• Match ≥0.90: Route to FAST-TRACK (Mechanical template fill | ₹0 LLM cost).
• Match <0.90: Escalate to Phase 3.

Phase 3: Legal RAG Engine & Grounded Generation (Claude 3.7 / Opus)

Queries NoticeDesk Vector DB for landmark High Court rulings & CBIC Circulars. Injects top-3 judgment excerpts into LLM prompt. Output is auto-cached for future ₹0 lookup. (Cost: ~₹3.80)

Phase 4: Assembly & Code Gateways (Sonnet + Code)

Sonnet writes overarching Facts & Prayer once. Code stitches paragraphs, renumbers, and enforces hard rules: No Empty Slots & Every Allegation Answered. (Cost: ~₹3.20)
