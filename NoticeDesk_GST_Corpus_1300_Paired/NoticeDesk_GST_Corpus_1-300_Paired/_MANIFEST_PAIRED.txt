NoticeDesk GST Paired Corpus — Notices + Drafts, Serials 1-300
=================================================================
Total pairs: 301 (300 unique serials; serial 205 has two variant drafts, 205 and 205A)

/drafts/   — the 300 original + 13 gap-filled taxpayer reply/appeal/letter drafts
/notices/  — matching source notices, REVERSE-GENERATED to pair with each draft

IMPORTANT — read before using for training or real work:
The /notices/ files are SYNTHETIC. They were generated by extracting the subject,
synopsis/facts, statutory section, form reference, and amount from each draft, then
reverse-composing a plausible departmental notice in the authority's voice. They are
NOT authentic notices. Quality varies:
  - ~96% of pairs pull a real allegation sentence from the draft's own Synopsis/Facts
    section and reframe it in notice voice.
  - ~4% (12 pairs) had no extractable synopsis and fall back to a generic templated
    allegation naming only the topic — flagged FALLBACK below.
  - For drafts that are themselves about a PROCEDURAL DEFECT in the department's own
    notice (e.g. unsigned DRC-07, notice issued by wrong officer), the reverse-generated
    notice can read awkwardly, since the draft's own text critiques the notice rather
    than stating a substantive tax allegation. Spot-check these before use.
Every notice uses bracketed placeholders ([____]) for DIN, reference number, date,
GSTIN and address — same convention as the original drafts.
