--- Issue #1 Grounds of Defence ---
[DEV - not CA reviewed] It is submitted that the discrepancy of Rs 5,00,000 in GSTR-2B was due to vendor delay in reporting, and all conditions of Section 16(2) were duly satisfied by M/s Apex Manufacturing Ltd.

--- Legal Submissions for Issue #1 ---
[DEV - not CA reviewed] Section 16(2)(aa) cannot be applied retroactively or punitively where genuine tax has been deposited by the supplier into the public exchequer.
