4. Key factual flags
DRC-01A contradiction: Doc 2's lead procedural objection — that the Department never issued Form GST DRC-01A — is directly contradicted by para 10 of the SCN, which records the DRC-01A dated 26.05.2026 and the noticee's own reply of 01.06.2026. Filing this ground as drafted risks the officer rejecting the entire reply's credibility on a checkable fact.
Wrong forum/officer: Doc 2 is captioned for the “Assistant Commissioner / Proper Officer... Division-Satpur”, whereas the SCN was issued and signed by the Additional Commissioner (Prev.), CGST & CX, Nashik Commissionerate, as a Centralized SCN. Doc 1's caption matches the signatory.
Business description: the SCN's own opening paragraph (an apparent drafting error, since it conflicts with the noticee's own statement at para 3.5 and the violation clauses at para 8) describes the noticee as a timber/wood retailer. Doc 1 treats this as an internal inconsistency to raise as a ground; Doc 2 instead adopts the wood/timber description as the noticee's actual business — which is not supported by the noticee's own statements on record.
Doc 1's quantification is independently verifiable: the Rs. 45,94,791 (Table A) and Rs. 1,96,24,576 (Table B) figures, and the Rs. 6,84,745 supplier-wise arithmetic inconsistency, all reconcile exactly against the SCN's own tables and narrative.
Doc 2 cites three cases (Bharti Airtel, Cosmic Radio v. CCE, Tamil Nadu Housing Board v. CCE) that do not appear in Doc 1's 19-entry, cross-checked authorities table. These should be independently verified against the reporter before filing.

6. Bottom line
Doc 1 is the more heavily fact-checked draft: its chronology, forum, and figures all reconcile against the SCN, and it turns several of the SCN's own drafting defects (undated notice, mismatched tables, wood/timber slip) into affirmative grounds. It is, however, still a working draft with placeholders to fill. Doc 2 reads as finished, but at least two of its grounds (DRC-01A, forum) conflict with the SCN's own record, and its case citations and business description need independent verification. If a single reply is to be filed, Doc 1's factual and legal scaffolding is the safer base, with the gaps ([INSERT]/[CONFIRM] items) completed before filing.
