3. STATUTORY GROUNDS & JUDICIAL PRECEDENTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. Compliance with Rule 8(4) & Rule 9(1) of CGST Rules, 2017:
     Under Rule 8(4), an applicant selecting "Consent" as the nature of possession of premises is legally required to upload a Consent Letter along with any one valid address proof (Electricity Bill / Property Tax Receipt). The Applicant has complied with both requirements.

3.2. Scope of Verification by Proper Officer:
     As established by the Hon'ble Delhi High Court in *Bimal Kumar Anraj v. Union of India* and *Subhash Chand v. Commissioner of CGST*, the Proper Officer's scrutiny for grant of GST registration is limited to verifying the authenticity of identity and possession of premises. The Officer cannot travel beyond statutory rules to demand title deeds where valid consent letters and municipal tax receipts are produced.
